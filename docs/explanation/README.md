# Explanation

> **Understanding-oriented** — Conceptual discussions that clarify and illuminate a topic.

Explanation documentation helps you understand *why* things work the way they do in Open KLara. It provides context, background, and deeper insight into the project's design and architecture.

## What belongs here

- Architecture overviews and design rationale
- Conceptual explanations of how components interact
- Background and context for design decisions
- Comparisons with alternative approaches
- Discussion of trade-offs

## Guidelines for writing explanations

- Focus on **understanding**, not on accomplishing tasks
- Provide **context** and **background**
- Discuss **alternatives** and **trade-offs** where relevant
- Connect concepts to the **bigger picture**
- It's OK to express **opinions** and reasoning
